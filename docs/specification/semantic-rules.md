# Semantic rules

- Absence of Prohibition does not imply Permission; absence of Permission does
  not imply Prohibition.
- Evidence, Provenance, and Credential are distinct.
- Recommendation and Instruction do not themselves create Authorization.
- Delegation is explicit and bounded; implicit delegation cannot manufacture authority.
- Memory, extensions, and descriptive domain semantics cannot manufacture authority.
- Frozen validity states are `valid`, `expired`, `revoked`, `superseded`, and
  `not-yet-valid`.
- Frozen Scope dimensions are spatial, temporal, and jurisdictional.
- Unknown or governance-overriding extensions fail closed as defined by the adapter.
