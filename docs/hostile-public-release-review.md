# Hostile public-release review

Reviewed from six perspectives: AI-safety researcher, semantic-web researcher,
security reviewer, open-source maintainer, skeptical academic, and competitor
seeking accidental proprietary disclosure.

- Contribution, layer boundaries, runnable commands, and falsification routes are visible.
- FIP is separated from domain ontology, GOCP, firewall evaluation, and gateway enforcement.
- Claims remain bounded; failed Q12-B/B2 experiments and B3 feedback limitation are explicit.
- The 26/24 vocabulary and 47-vector behavior are reproducible in two languages.
- Extensions cannot manufacture authority in the frozen reference behavior.
- No internal Git history, proprietary application code, customer material, secrets,
  private endpoints, personal contact data, or unpublished paper/patent drafts are included.
- Apache-2.0 exists in the source release; final release authority and NOTICE attribution
  still require human confirmation.

Known critique: `urn:fip:0.1:` is release-local, not a registered persistent web
namespace. The reference evaluator is intentionally minimal. Evidence is internal
and bounded, not outside validation, formal proof, or production certification.
